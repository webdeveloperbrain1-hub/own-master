AGENCY AGREEMENT (NON-EXCLUSIVE)
This Agreement is made on _____________, 20___ by and between _______________
(Name of Band/Artist) hereinafter referred to as “Artist” and
_____________________________ (Name of Agent) hereinafter “Agent” provides
consideration for Agent in exchange for the services and terms herein on its selected listof talent and its further consideration of the Agent's efforts to seek and find employment
specific to Artist’s brand of music for Artist in the music industry.
1. This is a non-exclusive agreement. Artists agrees that Agent may duly represent other
such musicians in the same manner that musician is represented without harm to Artist.
Artist may employ the services of another Agent. In such event, Artist will not be liableto Agent for any compensation from such outside assistance or services.
2. For all engagements in which Agent represents Artist and Artist is duly compensated,
Artist shall pay the agency a commission of ten percent (10 percent) on gross earnings forany employment in the entertainment field. Artist further agrees to pay the Agency
commissions on all residuals from employment that was secured by the Agency. No
agency commissions shall be payable on any of the following:
A. Separate amounts paid to Artist not as compensation but for travel or living
expenses incurred by Artist, and
B. Separate amounts paid to Artist not as compensation but a reimbursement for
necessary expenditures actually incurred by Artist in connection with Artist's
employment, such as damage to or loss of equipment, promotional materials, etc.
3. All earnings from those engagements in which Agent acted as representative on
behalf of Artist, shall be mailed or delivered directly to Agent. Agent, as required by lawin several states, shall deposit such earnings in its separate Trust Account. Within 14
business days, Agent shall be duly authorized to deduct the agreed upon fee of 10% and
send all remaining earnings to Artist with accompanying documentation regarding
disbursement, to the mailing address listed below.
4. In the event that earnings from engagements where Agent acted as representative for
Artist were inadvertently given to or sent to Artist, Artist shall, within 14 business days,
forward the due 10% owed to Agent.
4. Artist shall remain liable to Agent for all commissions still outstanding and deemed
owed to Agent despite any termination of this agreement.
5. Agent is hereby given the express permission to use the image, musical works,
biographical image, likeliness, other information and documentation as provided by
Artist for reasons of procuring employment in the music industry.

6. Artist is not obligated to accept any or all engagements presented by Agency. Artist,
for any reason, may refuse any engagement.
7. In the event that Artist breaches an agreement and fails to perform on a contract that
Agent procured for Artist, Artist shall remain liable to Agent for commission owed had
Artist not breached said contract. Further, Artist agrees to hold harmless and indemnify
Agent for any liabilities incurred by Artist under contracts procured and negotiated by
Agent.
8. The term of this Agreement shall be twelve (12) months from the date signed below.
Prior to natural termination, either Party may, in writing, for just cause, provide sixty (60)
days notice to the other party of its desire to terminate this agreement.
9. Neither party may assign or transfer any of its rights or duties under this Agreement.
10. Agent and Artist are contractors independent of one another and neither party’s
employees will be considered employees of the other party for any purpose. This
Agreement does not create a joint venture or partnership, and neither party has the
authority to bind the other to any third party without express written consent under a
separate agreement.
11. Artist agrees to keep updated and informed Agent of any changes in contact
information, whether it is phone, address, or email address. Failure of Artist to keep
informed Agent may result in the loss of income for both Artist and Agent.
