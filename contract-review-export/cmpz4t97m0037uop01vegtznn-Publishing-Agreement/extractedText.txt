agrees to pay the Author(s) jointly, during the original and renewal terms of the copyright
throughout the world as follows:
(a) In respect of regular piano copies sold and paid for at wholesale in the United Statesand Canada, royalties of FIVE (5) CENTS per copy.
(b) A royalty of FIVE (5) CENTS per copy of dance orchestrations sold and paid for in
the United States and Canada.

(d) The sum of ____________________ as and when the composition is published in any

(g) Except as herein expressly provided, no other royalties shall be paid in respect of the
composition.

receipt of said royalty payments.

Publisher and/or its associated, affiliated and subsidiary corporations by reason of the fact

composition or compositions hereto or hereafter acquired by the Publisher and/or its
associated, affiliated and subsidiary corporations.
