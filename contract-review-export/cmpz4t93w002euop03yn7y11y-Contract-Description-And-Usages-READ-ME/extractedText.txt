Contract Descriptions and Usages
1. AGENCY AGREEMENT – An agreement that allows an agency or individualto act on the artist’s or producer’s behalf to procure promotional engagements,
appearances, shows, and interviews.
2. ARTIST AND PUBLICIST AGREEMENT – An agreement that is used oncea recording artist (you) has finished recording an album and now aims to promotethe new album, as well as, promote a tour for the album. A publicist is the
professional an artist will hire get to help in many promotional endeavors. This
agreement solidifies the working relationship between the artist and publicist,
outlining the promotional duties the publicist will engage in on the artist’s behalf.
3. BAND PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT – An agreement to be used when
working in a duo, band, or group situation to ensure all terms and roles within the
band partnership are clearly defined. Additionally, this contract provides
provisions for how band members may be fired and/or quit, as well as, covers
publishing and copyright concerns.
4. BOOKING AGREEMENT (Comprehensive) – An agreement to present to
booking agents, club owners, or promoters for when booking a show or
performance. This agreement provides protection when booking a show outside
your home city and covers all bases for the booking, as well as guarantee
payment. Read over this agreement carefully. This agreement heavily protects
traveling artists/performers (you) and may cause some talent buyers to negotiate.
5. BOOKING AGREEMENT (Sales-Based) – An agreement to present to booking
agents, club owners, or promoters when booking a show or performance. Sales-
based pay depends highly on an artist/band’s ability to draw a crowd. This
agreement protects both parties, ensuring performance booking and payment
terms.
6. CO-PUBLISHER AGREEMENT – An agreement to use when 2 parties agreeto co-publish a musical composition(s) or collection(s). This agreement covers
copyright ownership and publishing receipt percentages, as well as, performance
rights assignments.
7. COPYRIGHT ASSIGNMENT – An agreement to use when a songwriter,
recording artist, or music producer professionally sells a copyrighted work to
another party. This agreement ensures the copyright transfer is official and secure.
Plus, it’s a great way to impress and show clients the songwriter, artist, or
producer (you) is a music industry professional.

8. PUBLISHING AGREEMENT – An agreement to use when an artist does not
have access to the resources and/or is not self-financed to promote one’s musicand thus chooses to work with a party who does have necessary resources, thus
signing a publishing deal with the artist. This publishing agreement provides for a
payment schedule, flat fees, and different royalty payments.
9. RADIO RELEASE AGREEMENT – An agreement to use when sending a
recording artist’s music (your music) to a radio station. If an artist’s music is
already copyrighted and published, often times the radio station may hesitate to
play the music without a radio release agreement. This agreement ensures the
radio station know that they are fully free to play the work.
10. RECORD RECORDING AGREEMENT – An agreement to use when a
company is willing to pay an artist to record an album. This is a nice, concise
agreement where terms of payment and promotion are flexible and may be
worked out easily. Pay special attention to the publishing portions of this
agreement. Be sure to get the terms that you want as an artist.
11. SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT – An agreement to use if an artist has an album
release, a tour, or even a single show coming up to assure the sponsors that their
dollars are safe and that the artist (you) are serious about promoting their product.
It also protects the artist, ensuring the sponsors act on their promises that are now
signed for.
